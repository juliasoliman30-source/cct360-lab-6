More Books to Check Out...

Talking at Night - Claire Daverley

Right person, wrong time? Will and Rosie never quite seem to stay apart from each other. Despite everything against them, they still find themselves together.

What Genres?
Romance, Fiction, Contemporary

Julia's Thoughts

I really wanted to like this book, but the ending was quite disappointing for me! It felt like I was trying to be convinced that they were the perfect match for each other, but they could not communicate.

Almond - Won-Pyung Sohn

A boy named Yunjae and how he navigates his world with alexithymia after a tragic loss.

What Genres?
Young Adult, Fiction, Contemporary, Coming-of-Age

Julia's Thoughts

I liked this book and read it so fast, but I really expected more from it. The later events did not seem consequential enough, and the ending was medicore to me.

Before the Coffee Gets Cold - Toshikazu Kawaguchi

A group of people meet at a café and have the opportunity to travel back in time to interact with their past selves.

What Genres?
Fiction, Contemporary, Magical Realism

Julia's Thoughts

I love this book, and I can deeply relate to a lot of the themes it explores and the characters. It is one of my favourites, and I have read the sequels as well.

The Cruel Prince - Holly Black

A young woman is kidnapped and forced to live in the fairy realm, where she must navigate political intrigue and dangerous magic.

What Genres?
Fantasy, Young Adult, Fiction, Romance

Julia's Thoughts

One of my first tastes of the YA fantasy genre! I actually quite liked this book, which surprised me because of my typical disdain for the romantasy genre. I also read the sequels as I enjoyed the tension between Jude and Cardan.

Convenience Store Woman - Sayaka Murata

A woman who has been working at a convenience store for years begins to question her life and the world around her.

What Genres?
Fiction, Contemporary, Literary Fiction, Satire

Julia's Thoughts

I surprisingly did not like this book. I hated basically all of the characters besides the main character, and I expected more based on how everyone was hyping it up. It still might be worth a read, but it was not enjoyable for me.

A Little Life - Hanya Yanagihara

A novel about the lives of four college friends and their struggles with trauma and relationships.

What Genres?
Fiction, Contemporary, Psychological, Drama, Tragedy

Julia's Thoughts

This book absolutely shattered me, but it is still one of my favourites to this day. I deeply related to Jude, and despite everyone's critiques about this book, I was not deterred at all. I cannot recommend though due to its heavily traumatic material, so please read content warnings.
